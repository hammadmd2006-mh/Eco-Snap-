## The EcoSnap Story & Vision

EcoSnap was built by **Alyssa X & Leo** during the *Ben's Bites Hackathon* to eliminate point-of-disposal confusion. By combining lightweight edge AI vision with gamified \*\*SnapPoints\*\*, EcoSnap empowers students to sort waste correctly in less than a second.

### YOLOv11 Edge AI

Quantized TensorFlow Lite model (\`best_float32.tflite\`) runs directly inside client browsers. Zero cloud latency and high privacy.

### SnapPoints Gamification

Instant point feedback (+10 per correct bin, +25 daily streak, +50 per 10 items) creates positive behavioral reinforcement.

### Smart Bin Synergy

Interfaces directly with 4-compartment automated hardware bins featuring 2.31s mechanical tilting actuation.
